# Workshop Assembly asset provenance

The three object-only images used by the Workshop Assembly sequence were generated for this redesign on 2026-08-18 with the built-in image-generation tool. They do not depict people and are not presented as photographs of a Pillars of Tech event.

The source brief requested one matched top-down robotics kit in three states—closed case, opened component kit, and assembled rover—on a cream technical-grid surface using the approved cream, ink, midnight, light-blue, and cobalt palette. It prohibited people, hands, faces, text, logos, watermarks, neon effects, and science-fiction styling.
